Astra Victorians · homeworkHow to fold your booklet, and a Victorian child's day
15POINTS
1. Fold in halfFold the sheet in half the long way, then open it. Fold it in half the short way, then in half again, so you have 8 little boxes.
2. Cut the slitFold in half the short way. Cut along the solid line from the folded edge to the middle: only across the two middle boxes.
3. Push it togetherOpen the sheet, fold it the long way, hold both ends and push them towards the middle. The slit opens into a diamond.
4. Fold the pages roundFold the pages round so the cover is on the front. Press the folds flat. Your eight-page booklet is ready.
