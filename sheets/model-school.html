Astra Victorians · homeworkWhat makes it Victorian, or modern?
15POINTS

Tick the features you build into your model. A good model shows at least five.

A Victorian school has…

  • red bricks and a steep slate roof
  • tall, high windows (so children could not look out)
  • a bell or a little bell tower
  • separate doors marked BOYS, GIRLS and INFANTS
  • separate playgrounds with a wall between
  • chimneys for the coal stoves
  • one big schoolroom with rows of desks
  • the teacher's house joined on
  • a toilet block outside in the yard
  • iron railings and a gate
My own ideas

A modern school could have…

  • big windows and lots of light
  • a hall for lunch, assemblies and PE
  • classrooms with tables in groups
  • a library and a computer area
  • a playground with climbing frames
  • a playing field and a garden to grow things
  • a ramp and wide doors so everyone can get in
  • solar panels on the roof
  • a quiet room for chilling out
  • something only your school has: a pool, an aquarium, a running track on the roof…
My own ideas

Tip

Build the box first, then the roof, then the small parts. Paint before you glue on windows. Let a grown-up help with any cutting.
