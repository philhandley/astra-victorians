Astra Victorians · homeworkDraw Queen Victoria in six steps
5POINTS

This is the young Queen Victoria, the way painters showed her just after she was crowned: hair parted in the middle and looped over her ears, a small crown, and a blue sash. Look at the real pictures, then draw lightly in pencil first.

1. An egg for a head

Draw an upside-down egg. Add a faint cross: the eyes go on the middle line.

2. Hair with a middle parting

A smooth cap of hair, parted in the middle, with loops that cover her ears.

3. A calm face

Two small eyes on the middle line, a little nose, and a small, serious mouth. Queens did not grin for portraits.

4. Neck and shoulders

A short neck, round shoulders and a wide neckline with lace. Add a necklace.

5. A small crown

A little crown sits on top of her hair: a band, points, and a few jewels. Earrings too.

6. Sash and colour

A wide sash from her left shoulder. Colour it blue, the dress white or black, the crown gold, the hair dark brown.

Astra Victorians · homeworkArt ideas: any materials you choose
5POINTS
Collage queenDraw her face, then dress her in scraps: fabric for the dress, tin foil for the crown, cotton wool or doily for the lace, a ribbon for the sash.
SilhouetteVictorians loved black paper silhouettes. Draw her side view (nose, chin, hair bun, crown) on black paper, cut it out and stick it on white.
Paint like a portrait painterPaint the background first, then the dress, then the face last. Mix a tiny bit of red into white for her cheeks.
Penny Black stampDesign a stamp with her head in profile, like the first ever stamp from 1840. Use the frame on this page.
Two VictoriasDraw her young on one half of the page and old (black dress, white lace cap) on the other half.
Salt-dough medalPress her face into a flat circle of salt dough with a pencil, bake it, and paint it gold like a coin.
My Penny Black

The Penny Black was the first stamp in the world. It showed the young queen's head from the side. Look at the real one, then draw her profile in the frame: forehead, nose, lips, chin, then the hair bun and a small crown at the back. Colour the background black and leave her face white.
