Astra Victorians · homeworkWhat is it like? Test it!
10POINTS

Choose eight things from your hunt. Test each one and tick every box that is true.

Hard? Press it with your thumb. Does it squash?
Bendy? Bend it a little. Does it bend or stay stiff?
Shiny? Shine a torch on it. Does it gleam?
See-through? Hold it up. Can you see through it?
Waterproof? Drip one drop of water. Does it soak in?
Magnetic? Touch it with a fridge magnet. Does it stick?

Did you know?

One of the very first man-made plastics was shown in Victorian times: Alexander Parkes showed "Parkesine" at a big exhibition in London in 1862. It was rare, so Victorian children hardly ever saw any plastic. Bakelite, the first plastic everyone used, came in 1907, six years after Victoria died.
