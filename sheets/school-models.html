This is a real school, built in 1852 for fifty children. Its architect, Joseph Clarke, drew the plans, and every window and door here is where he put it. It is 1/72 of the real size.

Architects today build schools from timber and glass, with gardens and playgrounds on the roof. This one has an orange top floor that sticks out over the front door, and a running track on its roof!

Ideas from schools that architects publish on Dezeen: timber cladding, big windows, a green roof with solar panels, a top floor that "cantilevers" out over the door, and places to play up high.
