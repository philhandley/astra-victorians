Look inside

Real buildings and machines, modelled from the plans. Spin them round, lift the roofs off, cut them open to see inside.

Fig. 01 · Monks Horton, 1852

A tiny school for 50 children

One room, one fire, a chalk floor and a bell you rang from inside. Ring it, then cut the school open to see inside.

 Fig. 02 · Wrenbury, 1879 and now

Our school, then and now

Wrenbury School from the 1897 map and a photograph, beside a new school on a plot the same size. Ring the bell, then watch the paper models fold.

 Fig. 03 · Osborne, 1840s

Queen Victoria's bathing machine

At its real size, on its grooved stone rails. Wind the winch to roll it down into the sea, then go for a swim with the dipper.
